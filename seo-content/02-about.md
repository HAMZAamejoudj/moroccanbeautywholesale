## Why We Started This Service

Moroccan beauty products are easy to love and easy to sell. Buying them is another story. Most suppliers are organised for large brands: minimums in the hundreds or thousands of units, bulk drums to repackage, and long email threads before you even see a price.

A concept store, a hammam, a riad or a small online shop doesn't need that. It needs a few cartons of products that are already labelled, packed and ready to use or sell, with a clear price and a quick answer.

That's what we set up: finished products, a 50-piece minimum, mixed orders, and a reply within 24 hours.
