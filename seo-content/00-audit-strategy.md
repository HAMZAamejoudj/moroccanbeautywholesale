# moroccanbeautywholesale.com - SEO Audit, Competitors and Strategy

Checked: https://moroccanbeautywholesale.com/en/ (homepage), October 2026.

## 1. Summary

The site looks professional and already has useful real facts (a 50-piece minimum, a reply-time promise, a street address, product origins). But in its current form Google is likely to see it as a **doorway site**: almost every call-to-action sends visitors to another domain, and the contact details belong to another brand. It also repeats certification claims that need proof, and it competes for exactly the same searches as several very similar sites.

## 2. Critical Issues

### 2.1 The site sends its visitors to moroccanorganica.com
- "See the Range", the three "View in catalog" links, "View Official Catalog" and even "Request a Quote" all link to moroccanorganica.com.
- "Request Pricing & Samples" opens an email to orders@moroccanorganica.com.
- The email in the footer and the Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links are Moroccan Organica's.

A site whose main job is to send visitors to another site is what Google's spam policies call a doorway. Fix: give this site its own catalog pages, its own quote form, its own email (e.g. orders@moroccanbeautywholesale.com) and, later, its own social profiles. No CTA should leave the site.

### 2.2 Claims that need proof
- "ECOCERT CERTIFIED", "USDA ORGANIC" (repeated 4 times in the scrolling banner), "100% Organic Certified", "21+ Certified ingredients".
- "1000+ Happy Partners", "hundreds of brands worldwide", "a leading Moroccan beauty ingredient supplier".
- "CRUELTY-FREE & VEGAN".
- Ingredient claims: "anti-aging", "skin-repairing properties".
Keep a certification only if you can show the certificate (holder name, number, scope, expiry). The USDA Organic seal has legal rules in the US. Replace numbers you cannot prove.

### 2.3 Canonical points to another URL
The page is served at https://moroccanbeautywholesale.com/en/ but its canonical is https://www.moroccanbeautywholesale.com/ (different host and different path). Choose one host (www or non-www), 301-redirect the other, and give every page a self-canonical.

### 2.4 Contradictory company facts
- Footer: "Lot 377 N°3/6 Sidi Ghanem Zone industriel, 40110 Marrakesh" and, just below, "Our factory is located in Agadir".
- Another of your sites (moroccocosmetic.com) says the argan oil is pressed in Marrakech.
Google and AI assistants compare facts across the web. If these sites belong to the same company, the address, the factory location and what you produce must be the same everywhere. If they are separate businesses, each needs its own real details.

### 2.5 Keyword stuffing
- A meta keywords tag with 25 keyword phrases (ignored by Google, but a spam signal).
- Paragraphs written for keywords, not readers: "Whether you need organic argan oil Morocco or prickly pear oil bulk supplier..."; "Ghassoul clay bulk, Rhassoul clay wholesale, Moroccan Nila powder wholesale, Aker Fassi powder wholesale...".
- The scrolling banner repeats the same 6 phrases 4 times in the HTML.

## 4. The Search Results You Are Entering

For "Moroccan beauty products wholesale" the first page is full of sites that look almost the same: moroccancosmeticsproducts.com, moroccanorganicproducts.com, soukcosmetics.com, moroccancosmeticswholesale.com, moroccanorganica.com, moroccobeautyproducts.com, moroccancosmetic.com and moroccocosmetic.com. Most use the same words: "trusted", "leading", "100% organic", "wholesale & private label supplier".

**Consequence:** one more generic "wholesale & private label supplier" site will not stand out. This site needs a different angle and a different audience.

> If some of these domains are also yours, tell me which ones: they are not competitors, they are part of the same cannibalization problem.

| Competitor | What they do well | Gap you can use |
|---|---|---|
| moroccanorganica.com (Organica Group) | Large catalog, product pages, blog, FAQ, stats | Bulk and private label focus; little for small shops, spas or hotels buying finished products |
| moroccocosmetic.com | Product pages per ingredient, "best-selling" section | Generic wholesale message |
| soukcosmetics.com | Huge content library, testimonials | Keyword-heavy, consumer content |
| zineglob.com | Certificates displayed, product-level private label | Thin education, template leftovers |
| moroccanorganicproducts.com | Marrakech, MOQ 50 pieces | Dated, "100% organic" claims |

## 5. Recommended Positioning

**moroccanbeautywholesale.com = ready-to-sell Moroccan beauty products, in small wholesale quantities, for shops, spas, hammams, hotels and online sellers.**

Not raw materials in drums (moroccocosmetic.com), not brand-building and regulations (moroccan-cosmetics.com): finished, labelled products that a business can put on its shelf, in its treatment room or in its guest bathroom, starting from 50 pieces.

| Site | Role | Main audience | Keyword family |
|---|---|---|---|
| moroccan-cosmetics.com | Private label & brand building | People launching a brand | private label, start a brand, regulations |
| moroccocosmetic.com | Marrakech factory & raw materials | Labs, manufacturers, distributors | bulk, OEM, ingredient names |
| **moroccanbeautywholesale.com** | **Ready-to-sell products, small quantities** | **Shops, spas, hammams, hotels, online sellers** | **wholesale ready-to-sell, spa supplies, hotel amenities, hammam kits, gift sets** |

## 7. Facts to Confirm Before Writing the Other Pages

1. Is the 50-piece minimum real? Per product or per order?
2. Do you reply within 24 hours on business days?
3. Real address and real factory location (Marrakech? Agadir? both, with what in each?).
4. Which certifications do you really hold, for which products (certificate numbers)?
5. "50+ countries": can you list the main ones?
6. Which ready-to-sell formats exist today (sizes, jars, sprays, kits, gift boxes, hotel minis)?
7. Do you already supply hotels or riads? Spas? (Real examples make these pages strong.)
