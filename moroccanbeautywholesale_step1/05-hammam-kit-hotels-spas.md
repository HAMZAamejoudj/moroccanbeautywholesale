A Moroccan hammam kit brings together the few products used in a traditional hammam. For a hotel, it is a memorable amenity. For a spa, it is the base of a signature treatment. For a shop, it is an easy gift that explains itself. The products are simple; what makes the kit work is choosing the right sizes and presenting the ritual clearly.

## The classic hammam kit

A traditional ritual uses five products, in this order:

1. **Black soap (savon beldi):** applied on warm, damp skin to soften it.
2. **Kessa glove:** a textured glove used to scrub away dead skin after the black soap.
3. **Rhassoul clay:** a mineral clay used as a body, face or hair mask.
4. **Rose water or orange blossom water:** to refresh the skin and to mix with the rhassoul.
5. **Argan oil:** a few drops to finish and moisturize.

Some kits add a towel (fouta), a small bowl for mixing the clay, or a bar of traditional soap.

## Adapting the kit to your business

### Hotels and riads
Guests use the kit once or twice, so **small sizes** are best: mini jars of black soap and rhassoul, small bottles of argan oil and floral water, and one kessa glove they can take home. A short card explaining the ritual turns the kit into a souvenir and a reason to talk about your hotel.

### Spas and hammams
For treatments, you need **professional sizes**: larger tubs of black soap and bags of rhassoul, bigger bottles of argan oil. Many spas also sell a retail version of the kit at reception, so clients can repeat the ritual at home.

### Shops and online stores
A **gift box** with medium sizes works best: enough product for several uses, presented in a box with the steps printed inside. Hammam kits sell especially well before holidays and as wedding or corporate gifts.

## Choosing scents

Offer one classic version and one scented version:

- **Classic:** plain black soap, natural rhassoul, rose water.
- **Fresh:** eucalyptus black soap, popular in spas.
- **Floral:** rose or orange blossom, popular for gifts and hotel guests.

## Presenting the ritual

Customers who know how to use the kit enjoy it more, and come back. Include a simple instruction card:

1. Warm up with steam or a hot shower.
2. Apply black soap, wait five minutes, rinse.
3. Scrub with the kessa glove.
4. Apply rhassoul mixed with rose water, leave a few minutes, rinse.
5. Finish with a few drops of argan oil.
